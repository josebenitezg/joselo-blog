I’m José Benítez — an electrical engineer, founder, and curious observer of how
technology changes the physical world and the people living in it.

My professional work in Physical AI lives at
[josebenitez.ai](https://www.josebenitez.ai). This site is the more personal
counterpart: essays, unfinished connections, and experiments that do not belong
inside a company-facing portfolio.

I write in English and Spanish. The point is not to perform certainty; it is to
make the thinking visible while it is still alive.
